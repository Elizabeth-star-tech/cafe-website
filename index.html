About Us

Our story

Savanna Brew started as a small coffee corner with one espresso machine and a big idea: a place where people could slow down over a good cup. Today we still roast and brew with the same care, and we have added a full kitchen so you can stay for breakfast, lunch or a late afternoon treat.

Everything on our menu is made fresh each day, from the pilau simmered slowly to the cakes baked every morning.

Our specialties

The dishes our regulars order again and again: slow-brewed masala tea, chef's pilau with fresh salad, and our chocolate cake baked every morning.

The experience

A warm, relaxed space with soft music, comfortable seating and a terrace for sunny days. Come alone with a book or bring the whole family.

Quality

Locally sourced coffee beans, fresh ingredients and no shortcuts. Everything on the menu is made from scratch each day.
